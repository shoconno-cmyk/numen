Numen

Jungian Archetype and Character-Arc Analysis for Screenplays

Frequently Asked Questions

Why archetypes? Why The Big Seven and Jung, and not just a reader's coverage?
Numen is designed to complement traditional coverage, not replace it. Jungian archetypes and arc analysis offer a unique lens on how a story is built and how it moves. Down the road, the goal is for Numen to work throughout the development process, tracking each draft and how its characters evolve.
Does Numen say whether a script is good?
No. It shows where archetypes appear and how characters change, with evidence. Whether that makes a good story is for the people reading it to decide.
Does a high archetype count mean my script will be a hit?
No. Archetypes are a lens on a script, not a predictor of box office or reviews.
Why not structure, pacing or coverage?
Other tools already do those well. Numen focuses on something they don't cover: archetypes, moment by moment.
How accurate is it?
On the demo script, 57 of the AI's 82 archetype calls held up on human review. The Story Report shows the AI's output and the human-reviewed version side by side, so you can compare them yourself.
Which AI does it use?
Claude, made by Anthropic. The pipeline is built so the AI can be swapped, including for one that runs on a studio's own machines.
Can I run it on my own script?
Not yet on your own. It's a working prototype shown on one script. Get in touch through LinkedIn for a read.

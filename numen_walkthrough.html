Jungian Archetype and Character-Arc Analysis for Screenplays

This walkthrough follows one feature-length script, Full of Grace, from the script’s PDF to its final Story Report.

What are The Big Seven?

The psychologist Carl Jung proposed that certain character patterns, which he called archetypes, recur across myths, stories and dreams in every culture and era, and that audiences recognize them both consciously and unconsciously: the hero, the wise guide, the dark double. The Big Seven are the archetypes that seem to appear most often in screenplays and films: six from Jung (Hero, Mentor, Shadow, Trickster, Great Mother and Persona), plus the Chorus from Greek drama. This walkthrough shows how Numen finds where each character presents as one of them, moment by moment.

1The Script Read

WHAT GOES IN:

The script PDF.

WHAT COMES OUT:

223 scenes, with every line labelled as a scene heading, action, character name, dialogue or a direction.

WHAT IT DOES:

The text is pulled out of the script PDF with its layout kept, split into scenes, and every line is labelled by what it is.

WORTH KNOWING:When two characters’ lines are printed side by side (dual dialogue), pulling the text from the PDF can scramble them. One of Trudy’s lines on page 123 lost her name and had words out of order; it was repaired by hand, checked against a second extraction.

2Finding the Moments

WHAT GOES IN:

The 223 labelled scenes.

WHAT COMES OUT:

460 moments, each holding its full text and the signals that marked where it begins and ends.

WHAT IT DOES:

Each scene is split into moments wherever the text shifts — a pause, a line that stops short, a sharp swing in tone.

WORTH KNOWING:These signals read the words one at a time, not what they amount to. A tone measure built on them was tested as an emotional wave across the script and eventually left out: it read Holly’s scream as close to neutral.

3The AI Labels the Archetypes

WHAT GOES IN:

Each of the 460 moments, one at a time, with its full text.

WHAT COMES OUT:

Each character in each moment gets one of The Big Seven, or no archetype. The AI also records how the audience sees the moment, how the character sees it, and what the character feels and wants. The run made 470 AI calls.

WHAT IT DOES:

The AI reads one moment at a time and says which of The Big Seven, if any, each character presents as; an answer outside the allowed terms is rejected and asked for again.

WORTH KNOWING:In one confrontation, the AI put John’s Hero call on the argument, not on the acts that follow. A Hero call needs the act itself, and review moved it.

4The AI Tracks Character Changes

WHAT GOES IN:

Every moment for each character with 3 or more moments.

WHAT COMES OUT:

524 judgments across 29 characters: whether each reaction fits the moment and fits the character, and where a character changes or shows a limit we haven’t seen before.

WHAT IT DOES:

The AI reads each character’s full arc first, names the traits that define them, then measures every moment against those traits.

It never checks a moment on its own; it waits until the character’s whole arc is in view. Note that it worked from human-reviewed archetype calls: 196 corrections had already been made to the previous stage.

WORTH KNOWING:The AI tracks John and Young John as two people, so it can’t connect the two halves of one life. The report shows them as one person, but these judgments were made separately.

5The Story Report

WHAT GOES IN:

The script text, and everything the AI produced in the stages above.

WHAT COMES OUT:

The Story Report: 4 sections, each tied to pages of the script.

WHAT IT DOES:

The AI writes a genre, logline and synopsis from the script alone, code assembles the rest from the earlier stages, and a check rejects wording that passes judgment on the script.

WORTH KNOWING:One stray letter in the AI’s synopsis was removed and a lost paragraph break restored. The repair is logged, and no words were changed.

Under the hood

The 10 real steps. The stages above group these; the human steps are the review track.

  1. Text extraction (stage 1). Run: pdftotext -layout. In: the script PDF. Out: fog_full.txt.
  2. Parsing and hand patches (stage 1). Run: build_fog_scaffold.py, parser.py, fog_hand_patches.py. In: fog_full.txt. Out: fog_parsed_scenes.json.
  3. Beat detection (stage 2). Run: build_fog_scaffold.py, beat_detector.py, signals.py, suspense_signals.py. In: fog_parsed_scenes.json. Out: fog_scaffold.json.
  4. Pass 1 tagging (AI) (stage 3). Run: fog_tagging_harness.py, llm_orchestration.py, integration.py, tagging_schema.py. In: fog_scaffold.json. Out: fog_tagged.json, fog_tagging_calls.jsonl.
  5. Human archetype review (the human review track). Run: by hand. In: fog_tagged.json. Out: fog_tagged.json, FOG_*_REVIEW.md.
  6. Pass 2 (AI) (stage 4). Run: fog_pass2_runner.py, pass2_orchestration.py. In: fog_tagged.json. Out: fog_pass2_calls/, fog_tagged.json.
  7. Human Pass 2 review (the human review track). Run: build_fog_turning_points_reviewed.py. In: fog_tagged.json, fog_pass2_calls/, FOG_*_REVIEW.md. Out: fog_tagged.json, FOG_*_REVIEW.md, FOG_PASS2_CLOSEOUT.md, fog_turning_points_reviewed.json.
  8. Development read (AI) (stage 5). Run: fog_dev_read_runner.py, report_guard.py. In: fog_full.txt. Out: fog_dev_read.json, fog_dev_read_call.json, fog_dev_read_archive/.
  9. Report data (stage 5). Run: report_data.py. In: fog_tagged.json, fog_full.txt. Out: fog_line_index.json, fog_report_model.json.
  10. Story Report (stage 5). Run: report.py, report_guard.py. In: fog_report_model.json, fog_line_index.json, fog_full.txt, fog_tagged.json, fog_turning_points_reviewed.json, fog_dev_read.json, FOG_*_REVIEW.md. Out: fog_story_report.html.

When Pass 2 ran. The first 196 corrections-log entries (commits 1fe11c8 to d294e1c) are human corrections, all in fog_tagged.json before the 524 Pass 2 drafts were applied (e26724d, partial; 8079f93, complete). The log now holds 847 entries.

A side step: plot facts. After the cold run, fog_plot_facts_runner.py pulled 3 settled plot facts from the script into fog_tagged.json. They were not available to the AI when it called the archetypes, and nothing in the report uses them.

Page and line numbers. The pipeline counts by PDF page (the title page first), by the script’s own printed page number (1–125), and by line in fog_full.txt (6665 lines). Holly’s scream is line 198: PDF page 5, printed page 4. This page and the report show printed pages only.

John and Young John. JOHN and YOUNG JOHN, and CHEYENNE and YOUNG CHEYENNE, are each shown as one character. The pipeline tracked the younger versions in the flashbacks as separate characters (FOG_COLD_RUN_FINDINGS.md finding 21). This page shows them as one person, as the report does.
